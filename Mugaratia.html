About

The Artist

Mugaratia has become a fixture of Zimbabwe's hip-hop scene, known for a contemporary style that pulls from traditional influences while staying rooted in modern production. His lyrics often reflect the culture and everyday experiences of life in Zimbabwe.

His 2024 album Pemurai Dzose blended hip-hop and contemporary sounds while touching on both personal experience and broader social themes. He followed it in 2025 with the Saladic Gangsta EP — a compact 17-minute project moving between indulgence, introspection, romance, and spiritual balance.

He's a regular on Zimbabwe's live circuit, having performed at Shoko Festival's Peace In The Hood concert in Chitungwiza, and returning to headline Shoko's Mash Up Night.

Discography

Debut & breakthrough album

PEMURAI DZOSE is Mugaratia's debut album and breakthrough project, released on 1 November 2024. Its sound blends Zim hip-hop, Afrobeat, trap influences, and traditional elements.

Debut album · Breakthrough album · 2024

PEMURAI DZOSE

The project’s major breakthrough included the hit “Sunday,” which reached number one on the Apple Music Top 100 Zimbabwe chart, alongside standout tracks such as “Tinashe” and “Ingredients.”
